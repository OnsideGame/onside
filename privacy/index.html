Onside Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026
App: Onside · Developer: Onside Games

This policy explains the information used by Onside's current Android online-play system and its website. Feature availability varies by build. It covers rooms, matches and reconnection, and spectating and tournaments in builds that include them.

Information used for online play

  • Player identity: your chosen player name and randomly generated player and session identifiers. The current online system does not require email-and-password registration.
  • Rooms and participation: room names and codes, public or private status, membership, host and team assignments, readiness, connection status, and playing or watching preferences.
  • Gameplay: moves, match state, scores, results, and, where available, tournament entries, brackets and progression. This information enables synchronized play and recovery after interruptions.
  • Connection information: the hosting and network infrastructure processes your IP address and connection information to connect your device and operate the service.

Player names and reconnection information are also saved on your device. Online play transmits relevant information to the game server. Choose a nickname and do not put your real name, email address, phone number, school or home address into player or room names.

How information is used and shared

The service uses this information to connect players, list rooms, manage participation, validate and synchronize gameplay, display results, support spectators, and restore interrupted sessions. Hosting and network providers process information needed to deliver those functions.

Other players in your room can see your player name, role, team, readiness and connection status, and relevant match information. Spectators can view the match they join. Public room details appear in the room browser. A private room code allows others with the code to attempt to join; share it only with intended participants.

There are no direct messages or voice communication. Player and room names are nevertheless visible to other participants.

Friends and future features

A feature allowing players to add one another as friends is planned. It is not part of the current inspected build, and that build does not collect a saved friends list. This policy will need to be updated when the feature is implemented to describe the identifiers and relationships stored, visibility, retention and removal controls. Planned features are not described here as current data collection.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

The inspected Android project and version 0.1.13 build have no identified integration for in-app advertising, analytics, third-party crash reporting, or in-app purchases. These services are not included in the current feature description. Device, operating-system, store and hosting providers may process information separately under their own terms. Any future integration requires an updated policy and corresponding disclosures.

Providers and website

The current online test service is hosted on DigitalOcean infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany, using a DuckDNS hostname. Information sent to the service may therefore be processed outside your country. The contact mailbox is provided by Google Gmail. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose provider may process visitor information such as IP addresses and access logs. This policy page contains no added analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms.

Storage, retention and deletion

On your device: saved preferences, player names and reconnection information remain until changed or the app's local data is cleared. You can clear local data using Android Settings → Apps → Onside → Storage → Clear data (wording varies by device). Clearing local data does not remove server-held records or email correspondence.

Online sessions: the current room service expires inactive rooms and disconnected sessions using inactivity rules. Active play or recovery can extend their lifetime. This is not a promise that every record disappears immediately when you disconnect.

Tournament recovery: tournament-enabled versions can save participant identities, session information, room and bracket data, and match state to server recovery files, including a previous snapshot. These copies can remain until replaced or removed. A fixed maximum retention period and a player-operated deletion tool for these files have not yet been established.

Infrastructure and correspondence: hosting-provider records are subject to the provider's practices. No separate fixed retention schedule has yet been established by Onside Games for operational records or messages sent to its mailbox. Server-data access and deletion requests are handled manually through the contact address below. Do not assume that uninstalling the app deletes server copies.

Security

The release client requires an encrypted WebSocket connection to the online service. Random session identifiers support reconnection; do not share them. The game server validates gameplay actions. No transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children and families

Onside is intended for all ages, including children under 13. The online features described above process player and connection information, including when a child plays. A general-audience content rating does not mean online play collects no information. Parents should help children choose non-identifying nicknames and avoid sharing personal details in room names.

Parents or guardians may contact us to ask about information associated with their child, request access or deletion, or report personal information included in a player or room name. We may ask for limited information to verify the request and locate the relevant records. Please do not send identity documents unless specifically necessary and requested. The current build does not provide an age-verification or parental-consent flow; this policy does not itself obtain parental consent.

Privacy inquiries

Onside Games' designated email address is Onsidegame2026@gmail.com. Messages sent there include your email address and whatever information you choose to provide. Do not send passwords, session tokens or unnecessary personal information.

Onside Games handles privacy inquiries and requests manually at this address. You may request access, correction or deletion of information associated with you, or with your child if you are their parent or guardian. Include the relevant player name, approximate date and room or tournament details, if known, so we can locate the information. Do not send session tokens. We will review the request, verify it where needed, and explain the action taken or any information we must retain and why. Rights and applicable response periods depend on your jurisdiction. There is no automated in-game server-data deletion control at present.

Policy changes

This page will be updated as the game and its information-handling practices change. The effective date identifies the latest version. Changes to friends, accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases or children's features must be reflected in the policy when introduced.
